## Priority
1. **Reforming the Judiciary, the rest of Ch. 22.** This is the only registered chapter whose text is incomplete (BA083).
2. **The appendices of Battles, Leveling the Playing Field and Reforming the Judiciary.** Several look like CJP speeches or tributes of 800+ words, so they would become new documents.
3. **Footnotes** for Battles, Judicial Renaissance and Liberty and Prosperity. They can be added later without changing the IDs.
4. **Front matter** for Leadership by Example, Leveling the Playing Field and Liberty and Prosperity, to confirm the publisher and the page numbers.
