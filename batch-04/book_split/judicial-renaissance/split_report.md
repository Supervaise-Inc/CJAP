## How the structure was worked out

- Every chapter heading from 1 to 22 was found in the text, in Contents order.
- The .docx formatting is inconsistent. Chapters 5–9 and 13–16 carry their chapter headings as bold text, and the others use Word heading styles.
- Section headings are every styled heading or fully-bold short line inside a chapter, all written as `##`. The source doesn't mark heading levels reliably, so they are not tiered.

## Decisions for the team

1. **Chapter 4 is by Anthony Gerald Toft, not CJP.** Toft is chief counsel for East Asia and the Pacific at the World Bank. The chapter is his lecture of August 24, 2005, in the CJ Davide Distinguished Lecture Series, and the Preface says it is "reprinted here". It is numbered as a chapter, but I classified it SKIP (written by someone else), which leaves a gap at Ch. 4. The later chapters keep the book's numbers and were not renumbered. **Please confirm.**
2. **candidate** (skipped, but a complete piece in CJP's own words of 800+ words)
   - **Preface** (1,757 words). This is his tenth-anniversary report: workload, output of more than 1,070 ponencias, and the plan of the book.
   - **Appendix A** (1,775 words). His speech launching Justice Vicente V. Mendoza's book at UP.
   - **Appendix C** (2,115 words). His speech to the Philippine College of Rotary Governors on excellence, ethics and eternity.
   - Appendix B, his tribute to Fr. Joaquin Bernas, is only 545 words, so it is not a candidate.
3. **long** (over 6,000 words; decide at B1 whether to split at the `##` sections)
   - Ch. 1: 6,663 body words, 32 sections.
   - Ch. 15: 11,267 words. It covers the original Decision, the Resolution reversing it, and the separate and dissenting opinions.
   - Ch. 16: 6,564 words.
   - Ch. 17: 6,176 words.
   - Ch. 22: 6,759 words, with 11 HRET case summaries.
4. **Collected speeches** (`collected_piece = yes`). Chapters 1–3 and 5–14 are speeches reprinted as chapters. The book doesn't print a source line or date for them, so `original_outlet` holds the occasion stated in each speech's own opening. `original_date` is blank, except that Ch. 14's summit dates (Feb 2–4, 2005) are recorded in its outlet. **B1 should check these 13 against the 153 speeches in the corpus**, especially Ch. 1 and Ch. 5 (Davide Lecture Series), Ch. 7 (LAWASIA), Ch. 11 (IBP convention) and Ch. 14 (Mining Summit). Chapters 15–22 are case summaries written for the book.

## Flag, do not fix
1. **Missing pages.** The Part II title page (pp. 241–242) and the Index (p. 511+) are not in the .docx.
2. **Missing footnote text.** The chapters carry footnote markers (`[1]`, `[2]`… on about 50 lines, most in Chapters 15–17), but no footnote texts are in the file. The source notes for the asterisks on Ch. 10 and Ch. 11 are also missing.
3. **Transcription artefacts in the skipped Preface.** The Isaiah 42:6-7 passage stops mid-line ("I, the Lord, have called you for the"). Paragraph 98 contains an inserted note, "[The seating diagrams are omitted from this transcription as requested.]" Both are in skipped parts, so no chapter file is affected. They do show that this .docx is a transcription, not a scan conversion.
4. **Text by someone else inside chapters.** The Part II chapters (15–21) summarize and quote other justices' opinions. They include full sub-sections headed "The Carpio Dissent", "The Morales Dissent", "Justice Puno's Opinion" and similar, and those sub-sections are more than a page in Ch. 15 and Ch. 16. Ch. 22 ends with a short poem by Atty. Noel Mapili, about 100 words and under a page.
