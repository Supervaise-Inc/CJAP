## Decisions for the team
1. **Chapter 9 is by Atty. Ismael G. Khan Jr.** He was then head of the SC Public Information Office. The chapter is his *Philippine Daily Inquirer* commentary of May 30, 2004, summarising CJP's Chile lectures, and the Part II Introduction calls it the "Prologue to Part II".
   - It was first classified SKIP under the other-author rule, which left a gap at Ch. 9. **Corrected on 24 Sep 2026 at the team's request: it is now a CHAPTER**, so the numbering runs 1–21 unbroken and the book's own structure is preserved.
   - The document keeps the printed byline "by Ismael G. Khan Jr." as its first body line, and CJP's own headnote (the paragraph beginning "This commentary was printed in the May 30, 2004 issue…", about 90 words) follows it.
   - `collected_piece = yes`, `original_date = 2004-05-30`, `flags = by-another-author`. **B1 must decide whether a document not in CJP's voice belongs in the corpus**; if it does, its theme and rights need the other author's name against them.
2. **candidate:** the **Preface** (2,393 words). It is his ninth-anniversary report: the three parts, the new HRET chairmanship, committee work, and why he chose the Psalm epigraph.
3. **Part introductions were skipped, please confirm.** Each Part has a short, unnumbered Introduction in CJP's own words (269, 234 and 129 words). They mostly describe what the following chapters contain, so I did not treat them as rule-3 author introductions. If the team wants them, they are best attached to the start of the first chapter of each Part rather than made into separate documents.
4. **long** (over 6,000 words; decide at B1 whether to split at the `##` sections)
   - Ch. 15 *Francisco*: 10,314 body words, 25 sections, most of them one justice's separate opinion each.
   - Ch. 16 *ITF v. Comelec*: 9,736 words.
   - Ch. 21 on capital offenses: 7,018 words, 17 sections, each a separate doctrine.
5. **Speeches** (`collected_piece = yes`, occasion from the text in `original_outlet`).
   - Part I, Ch. 1–5 and 7–8, are speeches. The Part I Introduction says Ch. 1–5 are "selected speeches" from the Court's "Chamber-to-Chamber" and "Justice-to-Justice" campaigns.
   - Part II, Ch. 10–14, are the five lectures he gave in Chile on March 1–5, 2004.
   - No dates are printed per chapter except the Chile series. **B1 should check all 12 against the speeches in the corpus.**
