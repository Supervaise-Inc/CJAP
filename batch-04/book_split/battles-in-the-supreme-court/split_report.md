## How the structure was worked out
- The front matter (cover, jacket flap, epigraph, frontispiece, portrait, title page, copyright and dedication, a photo section, and the Contents) exists only as scanned page images at the start of the .docx. I read the Contents from those images (pp. xvii–xix) and took the printed page numbers from it. The text has no page numbers or running headers, so **start/end pages come from the Contents**. Each end page is the next part's start page minus 1.
- The chapter headings in the text match the Contents: Chapters I–XI in order, with no gaps.
- The .docx heading styles are unreliable. Some section titles are styled Heading 1 or Heading 3 and others are plain text. Section headings were therefore taken from the Contents and the printed text:
  - `##` marks a section listed in the Contents.
  - `###` marks a lower-level printed sub-heading that is not in the Contents (for example the "First/Second/Third Round" parts of Ch. I, the speech's sub-headings in Ch. VI, and the dissent's sub-headings in Ch. VIII).
  - A heading printed across two lines is joined into one.
- The text was not changed. Footnotes stay where the .docx puts them, together with their `____` separator lines. Because this is a scan conversion, that is often mid-paragraph at the old page breaks. OCR errors are left as they are.
