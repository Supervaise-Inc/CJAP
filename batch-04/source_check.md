## Flags
1. **Heading levels.** Five books use a second heading level inside chapters (Battles, Judicial Renaissance, Leveling the Playing Field, Love God Serve Man, Reforming the Judiciary). Those lines are kept as `###`, under their `##` section, rather than flattened, so the structure the book prints is preserved.
2. **Footnotes are inline, not under "## Notes".** Battles in the Supreme Court prints its footnotes at the foot of each page, and the conversion keeps them where they fall in the text, with their `____` separator lines. Moving them to the end would reorder the author's text, which B0/B2 must not do. The other books have no footnote texts at all (see `book_split/SOURCE_GAPS.md`).
3. **Incomplete text: BA083** (Reforming the Judiciary Ch. 22) ends mid-sentence, because the source .docx is truncated. It is the only file whose text is known to be incomplete.
4. **Passages not by CJP, longer than a page, kept inside their chapter:** BD038 (APJR Executive Summary), BD034 (the Davide Watch statement), BA059 (House deliberations), BB015 (statute and DOLE texts), BA063 (a Comelec report), and the 22 Love God Serve Man files, each of which opens with a short editor's note by Isagani Yambot.
5. **OCR damage was not repaired.** Items such as "Tam sure", "4! separate opinions" and "Equatorial Reality v. Mayfair Theater" (for "Realty") stay as printed; correcting them needs the printed book.
6. **Source lines.** Page ranges are included only for the three books whose Contents gives page numbers (Battles, Judicial Renaissance, Reforming the Judiciary). For collected speeches the original occasion is appended in brackets.
